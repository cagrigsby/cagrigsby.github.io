# About Me

My name is Chris, welcome to my website. Initially I created this site to help track my progress as I built my career in cybersecurity, and I intend for now, there are [some](/oscp_notes.md) [relics](/writeups) of that purpose. One [blog post](/2024/08/10/cybersecurity-PATH.html) in particular outlines my journey getting started with no technical experience during the COVID-19 pandemic and breaking into the field. I suspect it would give some clues into what kind of person I am. 

That said, I've worked on a lot of other projects in my life, and I'm hoping to spend a little more time writing about them. It's not a fresh take, but the Internet has been condensed into a small number of huge websites, which has twisted the original promise of an open web. As someone who spent some time on the Internet before social media took over, I have an appreciation for the small web. As I get older now, I find that myself feeling grateful for the people who contributed to that feeling of discovery, and I feel responsible to participate. And I like to write. It will take me a little bit of time to start producing again, but anyway, that's what I'm doing here.
